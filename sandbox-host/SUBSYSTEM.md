name: resident-workspace-process-host
definition: >
  Realizes an admitted workspace release as managed processes resident in the
  enactor's actual environment and publishes an endpoint only after witnessed
  listening. A source binding identifies intended content and placement; it
  cannot establish the current environment or authority that admits serving.
  Hosted bindings must match the actual sandbox. Machine bindings additionally
  match an independently supplied current managed session, writer generation
  and canonical source root. Public identities contain no private credential
  and cannot replace the supervisor's current admission.
  Process custody is isolated by actual environment and, for machine work,
  installation, session, workspace generation and root before component.
  Replacement, restart, readiness and retirement act only within that exact
  custody. A foreign port or predecessor is not permission to signal another
  environment's work. Existing data survives ordinary process replacement.
  Managed machine admission and restart stop when current session standing
  expires or closes. Owned process cessation is witnessed, with incomplete
  drain retained rather than claiming release or deleting authored source.
  Machine readiness requires positive operating-system listener ownership;
  an unrelated listener cannot be credited merely because an owned process
  is alive. Inaccessible ownership remains unavailable. This is finite
  observed readiness, not perpetual endpoint identity. A retained numerical
  process identity alone cannot authorize a later signal or prove a live
  incarnation. Machine cessation is requested through the exact retained
  supervisor custody, and only that live supervisor acts on its own group.
  Session observation survives carrier restarts: equal ready revisions retain equal
  meaning, regressions refuse, and observed expiry or closure cannot be erased
  by a later revision of the same session.
  Local reachability is explicitly loopback; hosted reachability retains its
  declared sandbox ingress. Neither a configured port nor a running process
  alone establishes the release's witnessed serving identity.
  Managed local enactment additionally owns a complete retained launch census
  for each admitted effect, independently of its serving endpoint projection.
  Every attempted launch is recorded before spawn, including failed startup,
  replacement and an attempt whose actual birth remains uncertain. Exact
  process ownership and private control custody stay local; public identities
  locate records but cannot authorize signaling. A previous live incarnation
  remains owed until its actual cessation is witnessed, even when a later
  deployment succeeds. Ordinary release success may retain continuing serving.
  Supported nonsecret planning names complete target and phase coverage before
  credentials or effects. Historical observation and cessation use the same
  immutable selected realization and original private tuple and launch custody.
  They remain bounded cleanup after ordinary session admission expires, without
  reviving that session, restarting work or replacing a process. Observations
  append with exact causal predecessors; no current record can erase a prior
  possibly live attempt. Complete sealed census and positively witnessed whole
  group cessation, or positive non-send evidence, alone establish drain.
  Missing, unreadable, foreign or conflicting custody is unavailable, never
  silently omitted as it may be from a display-only endpoint projection.
  Nonsecret capability planning receives exact selected target and material
  identities without raw secret-bearing state or credential access. It cannot
  replace the enclosing worker's actual realization selection or resource-engine
  evidence. Each target census gives new and referenced launch memberships one
  contiguous exact ordinal sequence. Membership provenance is immutable;
  additions stop at the sealed frontier, while later causal observations may
  witness cessation without rewriting history. Referenced launches retain
  their original owner and cannot be relabeled as this effect's new work.
  Machine entry requires the actual controller's finite admission projected
  onto a shared suspend-inclusive clock and exact boot domain. Private custody
  carries its absolute cutoff; workers and surviving supervisors compare that
  same cutoff without rebasing on restart, file reread or wall time. Missing
  or changed clock ground refuses further entry while preserving historical
  stop responsibility. Every child and restart attempt is retained before
  spawn under the same seal order; descendants remaining in one independently
  controlled group remain one public group obligation.
  An original managed effect retains its own finite boot-clock cutoff as
  well as session standing. Both must permit every ordinary launch or restart;
  renewing the session cannot extend the original effect. Historical observe
  and stop retain separate finite custody without reopening launch admission.
  Machine process evidence assumes the declared cooperative controller and
  host substrate. Private file modes and control secrets do not isolate
  custody from a malicious operator or same-principal authored process. The
  actual selected owner invocation supplies complete evidence to that trusted
  controller; public identities and caller-authored success documents are not
  owner observations. This boundary makes no independent attestation claim.
  Duplicate ready admission preserves its entire original carrier at an equal
  renewal revision. Local closure may revoke that exact session at the same
  revision while preserving its original clock and attribution. Terminal
  custody survives carrier restart and prevents every later ready revision
  from reopening the same session; it proves no consequence has ceased.
  Machine serving consumes the exact admitted resident build material and
  actual realized runtime associated with the original successful build.
  Binding interpretation belongs here; generic platform custody does not
  manufacture realization-specific content. The binding must agree with
  independently supplied current placement and retained runtime evidence.
  Changed, missing or foreign material refuses before launch, and artifact
  admission alone proves neither current serving nor independent host trust.
